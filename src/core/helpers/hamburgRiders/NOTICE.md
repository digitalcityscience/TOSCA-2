# HamburgRiders module — provenance

The visual language, colour constants, GTFS classification and animation logic in this folder are adapted from
**hamburgriders** (MIT, © 2026 Bert Temme, see `LICENSE-hamburgriders.txt`), itself forked from
[utrechtriders](https://github.com/bertt/utrechtriders) and inspired by
[londonriders](https://anita.garden/londonriders/) by anita.

Data in `public/hamburgriders/data/` is the pre-built extract of the HVV GTFS timetable published on the Hamburg
transparency portal (`meta.json` records feed publisher, version and validity). It is a **scheduled timetable for
one service date, not live vehicle data**. Basemap tiles (dark style) are CARTO Dark Matter © CARTO © OpenStreetMap
contributors.
